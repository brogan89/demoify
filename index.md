# Demoify

**GitHub for songs.** Demoify is a versioned demo-sharing platform for musicians —
share tracks publicly or privately under one permanent link, push new versions
without breaking that link, and collect feedback in timestamped comments. It's
open source and self-hostable, and public instances can federate their tracks
into a shared Explore feed.

This site is the project's knowledge base: feature deep-dives, operational
runbooks, and planning history. Source lives in [`docs/`](https://github.com/brogan89/demoify/tree/main/docs)
and this site is rebuilt automatically whenever those files change.
