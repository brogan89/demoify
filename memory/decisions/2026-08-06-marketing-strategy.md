# 2026-08-06 — Adopt operating marketing strategy; drive to launch by week 4

**Status:** Adopted 2026-08-06 (decisions confirmed with Brogan same day).

**Decision:** [context/marketing-strategy.md](../context/marketing-strategy.md) is Demoify's operating marketing plan. It supersedes `docs/Demoify_Marketing_Plan.pptx` (July 2026), which remains as reference.

**Key choices:**

- Free, staggered public launch **Sep 1–4, 2026** (Show HN → Product Hunt → music subreddits/IMF). Do not gate launch on Stripe.
- Seed **100–150 real tracks by white-gloving 25–40 artists** (replaces the deck's "300+ songs"). Hard gate: ≥100 real tracks in Explore by Aug 27 or the launch slips one week.
- **OG images + signup attribution are preconditions** to any outreach or paid spend — neither exists today.
- The **open-source/self-host/federation wedge** (r/selfhosted, awesome-selfhosted, directories) is promoted to a first-class channel alongside the musician channels.
- Paid (Phase 2, NZ$200–500/mo, micro-creator sponsorships first) only after: attribution trusted **and** ≥100 activated artists with ≥20% wk-4 return **and** one organic channel at ≥10 signups/wk.

**Context:** Product is deployed and stable on demoify.app but effectively unlaunched — all content self-seeded, zero outside users, Stripe off, no attribution/sitemap/OG imagery.
